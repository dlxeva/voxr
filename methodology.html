METHOD & BOUNDARY

方法与边界 / Methodology

Voxr 基于 SRT 文本和时间轴做合成旁白的制作复盘；它帮助定位值得重听、重写或重配的片段，而不输出声学、情绪或可懂度的自动结论。

Voxr reviews synthesized voiceover from SRT text and timing. It identifies clips worth replaying, rewriting, or regenerating; it does not produce automatic acoustic, emotion, or intelligibility conclusions.

当前测量什么 / What it measures

计算字幕覆盖、完整时间线、字幕间隔、中文字符数和英文词数，并由此显示 CPM、WPM、覆盖外时间占比、间隔频率和相邻语速变化。“字幕覆盖”不等于真实开口，“覆盖外时间”不等于静音。

It calculates subtitle coverage, the full timeline, cue gaps, Chinese characters, and English words, then displays CPM, WPM, time outside coverage, gap frequency, and neighboring rate changes. Coverage is not actual spoken duration; time outside coverage is not silence.

场景预设 / Delivery presets

用途区间来自当前团队的制作复盘规则，作用是标出值得人工复查的片段。它们不是研究结论、行业标准或自动判定；最可靠的用法是比较同一用途下的多个生成版本。

Purpose ranges are current team production-review rules that flag clips for human review. They are not research conclusions, industry standards, or automatic judgments; compare multiple renders made for the same purpose.

Audio QC Lite

用户确认拥有授权后，可本地导入音频并查看 PCM 波形、样本峰值、RMS 能量、接近满刻度样本、时长差和低能量字幕区间。文件不会上传。

After authorization confirmation, users can locally inspect PCM waveform data, sample peak, RMS energy, near-full-scale samples, duration difference, and low-energy cues. Files are not uploaded.

它不报告 LUFS、True Peak、音高、情绪、可懂度或音色相似度。

It does not report LUFS, true peak, pitch, emotion, intelligibility, or voice similarity.

方法来源与参考 / Design lineage and references

Voxr 借鉴字幕时间轴质检的常见做法，再将问题时间码转成合成旁白工作流所需的重配动作与通用 JSON。它是独立实现，不复制第三方网页工具的代码。

Voxr is informed by common subtitle-timeline QC practice, then translates review timecodes into regeneration actions and generic JSON for synthesized-voice workflows. It is independently implemented and does not copy third-party web-tool code.

  1. Yuan & Church (2021), ICASSP — Mandarin rate and prosody depend on corpus and context.
  2. Pitt, Szostak & Dilley (2016) — Contextual rate changes can affect speech perception.
  3. Lai & Dilley (2016) — Contextual rate can affect word perception in Mandarin.
  4. Breznitz (1990) — Pauses and voiced time change under faster reading.
  5. Mahr, Rathouz & Hustad (2024) — Speaking rate and intelligibility show a speed–accuracy trade-off in children.

文献支持测量方向，不直接推出 Voxr 的任何 CPM、WPM 或间隔参考区间。 / The literature supports the measurement direction; it does not directly derive any Voxr CPM, WPM, or gap range.

已知限制 / Known limitations

  • SRT 可能与真实说话内容不完全一致。 / SRT timing and text may not match the actual performance.
  • 重叠不能解释为多人、双语或时间码错误中的任何一种。 / Overlap cannot be attributed to concurrent speakers, bilingual subtitles, or a timing error.
  • 中英混合内容不生成单一的“正常”判断。 / Mixed-language material receives no single “normal” judgment.
